Local Search Marketing That Gets Businesses Found Nearby

Why Local Search Matters

Most local buying decisions start with a phone search, not a browser bookmark. Someone searches "near me," opens Google Maps, or increasingly just asks an AI assistant to recommend one. Whoever shows up complete, credible, and close wins that moment, regardless of how good the losing business actually is.

Google Business Profile

Your Google Business Profile is often the first, and sometimes only, thing a potential customer sees before deciding whether to call. An incomplete profile, missing hours, no photos, an unanswered question, costs you customers before they ever reach your website. We build out and actively manage your profile so it works as hard as your homepage does.

Reviews and Citations

Reviews are the local trust signal both customers and algorithms rely on most: more reviews, and more recent reviews, correlate directly with map ranking and with whether an AI assistant is willing to recommend you. Citations, your business name, address, and phone number listed consistently across directories, do the same work in the background, confirming to search engines that you're a real, established business at a real location.

Local Content and AI Recommendations

Generic service pages rank poorly against competitors who write specifically about the neighborhoods, landmarks, and local context their customers actually search for. Location-specific content gives search engines and AI platforms the local relevance signal they need to recommend you for a nearby search instead of a generic one.

Questions About Local Search

What's the single most important local SEO factor?

An accurate, complete, and actively managed Google Business Profile. It's usually the first thing a nearby customer sees, and an incomplete or outdated one costs you customers before they ever reach your website.

How many reviews do I need?

There's no magic number. A steady stream of recent reviews consistently outperforms a large but stagnant total, and recency and response rate matter as much as raw volume for both ranking and AI recommendations.

What are citations, and do they still matter?

A citation is your business name, address, and phone number listed consistently across directories. They still matter as a trust and verification signal, even though their direct ranking weight has declined over the years.

Can an AI assistant recommend my business without me doing anything?

Unlikely. If your online presence is thin or inconsistent, an AI system has nothing solid to cite and will recommend a competitor with clearer, more consistent local signals instead.

Do I need a physical location for local SEO to work?

No. Service-area businesses can still optimize for local search, though certain features, map pack prominence in particular, work differently without a storefront customers can visit.
