Questions About IT Staff Augmentation

What is IT staff augmentation?

IT staff augmentation allows a company to add qualified external technology professionals to its existing team for a specific skill, project, workload, or period of time without immediately adding permanent headcount. The professionals work alongside your team while giving you greater flexibility to adjust resources as requirements change.

What is the difference between staff augmentation and outsourcing?

With staff augmentation, outside professionals typically become an extension of your existing team while you retain control over the work and priorities. With project outsourcing, responsibility for delivering a defined project or outcome is generally assigned to an outside provider. SilverXis provides both technology talent and software development capabilities, allowing the engagement to be structured around what the work actually requires.

How does SilverXis qualify IT candidates?

We begin with the actual requirements of the role rather than simply searching for resumes containing the right keywords. Candidates are qualified based on relevant experience, technical capabilities, communication, and the requirements you establish for the position before being presented to your team.

How quickly can SilverXis provide qualified IT candidates?

Timing depends on the role, required skills, experience level, location, availability, and hiring requirements. Clearly defining the role at the beginning allows recruiting to focus on qualified candidates rather than candidate volume, helping reduce time spent reviewing people who don't meet the requirements.

Can we hire a SilverXis contractor permanently?

Contract-to-Hire provides a path for companies that want to work with a professional before making a permanent hiring decision. SilverXis also provides Direct Placement when the requirement is a permanent hire from the beginning.

What types of IT professionals can SilverXis provide?

SilverXis recruits across software development, cloud, infrastructure, DevOps, data, AI and machine learning, QA, automation, business analysis, project management, architecture, and other specialized technology disciplines. Candidates are qualified against the requirements of the specific role rather than selected from a predetermined list of job titles.
