SEO That Helps the Right Customers Find You

Technical SEO

Technical SEO is the plumbing: site speed, mobile performance, secure and crawlable URLs, clean site architecture, and structured data that tells search engines exactly what they're looking at. Get this wrong and even great content struggles to rank, because search engines can't properly access or understand it.

On-Page SEO

On-page SEO is how each individual page communicates what it's about: title tags, headings, internal links, and content structured around the terms your customers actually search. It's the difference between a page that happens to mention your service and a page built specifically to rank for it.

Content

Search engines rank pages that thoroughly answer a real question over pages that just mention a keyword. We build content around the specific questions your customers are asking, in the language they use to ask it, so each page earns its ranking instead of chasing one.

Entity Signals

Search engines and AI platforms don't just read pages, they build a model of who you are as a business: what you do, where you operate, and how credible you are. Entity signals, consistent business information, structured data, and clear relationships between your content, all feed that model. Weak or inconsistent signals make you harder to trust and easier to overlook.

Authority

Authority is why one business ranks above another with similar content. It's built through backlinks, mentions, reviews, and a track record of publishing genuinely useful content over time. There's no shortcut to authority, but there is a wrong way to spend a marketing budget trying to fake it.

Local SEO

For businesses that serve a specific area, local SEO is often the highest-leverage part of the entire strategy: it's how you show up when someone nearby searches for exactly what you offer, in Google Maps, in the local pack, and increasingly in AI-generated local recommendations.

Questions About SEO

What does SEO actually include?

Technical SEO, on-page optimization, content, entity signals, and authority-building, working together rather than as separate line items. Doing one well while ignoring the others leaves an easy opening for a competitor.

How is SEO different from paid ads?

SEO earns organic rankings that keep working after you stop actively paying for the work; paid ads stop the moment you stop paying for them. Most businesses need both, but SEO is the asset that compounds.

How long does SEO take to work?

Technical fixes can show measurable movement within weeks. Competitive rankings for the terms that actually drive business typically take two to six months, since they depend on authority signals that build over time.

Does SEO still matter now that people search on AI platforms?

Yes, it matters more, not less. SEO is the foundation AEO and GEO build on: the entity clarity and authority you earn through SEO is exactly what makes an AI platform trust you enough to cite you.

What's the biggest SEO mistake businesses make?

Chasing rankings for keywords that don't actually match buying intent, or treating SEO as something bolted on after the site is built instead of designed in from the start.
