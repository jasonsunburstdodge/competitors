Questions About SilverXis

What does SilverXis do?

SilverXis is a technology solutions company providing Custom Software Development, IT Staff Augmentation, and Digital Marketing. These capabilities can work independently or together depending on the business problem being solved.

Where is SilverXis headquartered?

SilverXis is headquartered in Irving, Texas, in the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex, with additional delivery capabilities in India and clients across the United States and internationally.

How long has SilverXis been in business?

SilverXis was founded in 2005, giving the company more than two decades of experience helping businesses apply technology to changing operational and growth challenges.

What types of businesses does SilverXis work with?

SilverXis works with businesses that need to build or improve technology, add specialized IT expertise, or strengthen how they reach and convert customers. Engagements can range from a specific project or role to broader technology and growth initiatives.

Why does SilverXis offer software development, staffing, and digital marketing together?

Because business challenges often cross those boundaries. A software initiative may require specialized talent. Growth may expose limitations in existing systems. A new digital product may also need a strategy for reaching its market. Having all three capabilities allows SilverXis to look at the larger business objective instead of viewing every challenge through a single service.
