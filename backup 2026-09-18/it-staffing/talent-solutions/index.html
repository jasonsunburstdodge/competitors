Questions About IT Talent & Staffing Solutions

What types of IT professionals can SilverXis provide?

SilverXis provides technology talent across software development, cloud and infrastructure, DevOps, data and analytics, AI and Machine Learning, QA and automation, business analysis, project management, architecture, technology leadership, and other specialized disciplines.

Candidates are qualified against the requirements of the specific role rather than relying solely on job titles or keyword searches.

What is the difference between contract, contract-to-hire, and direct placement?

Contract staffing adds talent for a defined period or ongoing need without immediately creating a permanent position.

Contract-to-hire begins as a contract engagement with the possibility of transitioning the professional into a permanent employee.

Direct placement is designed for positions you intend to fill permanently from the beginning.

What is a dedicated IT team?

A dedicated IT team brings multiple technology professionals together around a project, product, capability, or ongoing requirement.

The team can combine different technical disciplines based on the skills, scope, collaboration, and delivery model the work requires.

What is the difference between onshore, nearshore, and offshore IT staffing?

Onshore talent works within the same country as the client. Nearshore talent works from a nearby country or region, often providing greater time-zone overlap. Offshore talent works from more distant global delivery locations.

The right model depends on expertise, collaboration, coverage, availability, scalability, and economics.

Can SilverXis provide both individual IT professionals and complete teams?

Yes. Staffing can be structured around one specialized role, multiple professionals, permanent positions, or a dedicated technology team.

The appropriate structure depends on what you need to accomplish, the capabilities already available internally, and where additional expertise or capacity is required.
